Common Questions

What does the free brand audit actually involve?

A 30-minute conversation — no template, no prepared slide. We look at what you're doing, where you're positioned, and give you an honest read on where the brand communication is strong or leaking. You'll leave with at least one clear action.

How long does a typical engagement last?

It depends entirely on the scope. A strategy sprint might be 2–4 weeks. A full launch partnership could run 6–12 weeks. We scope before we start, and we're clear about what's included.

Do you work with early-stage brands?

Yes — and often this is where the thinking matters most. Pre-launch or early-stage brands benefit from getting the strategy right before spend happens, not after. Some of our most impactful work has been at this stage.

Do you work with brands outside India?

Yes. While we're India-rooted, we've worked with brands entering and operating in global markets. The thinking is global — the application is contextual.

What if we don't know what service we need?

That's exactly why the first call exists. Tell us what's happening and we'll tell you what we think the problem actually is — and whether we're the right people to help with it.

Who do I actually work with at Boney for Brands?

The principal consultant — directly. Every call, every brief, every deliverable. Specialists are brought in when the work requires it, but they're always briefed properly and supervised throughout.
